# Security policy

Do not open a public issue for a vulnerability in Inferrex, these review
skills, or evidence that may expose credentials, customer content or security
controls.

Use GitHub's private vulnerability-reporting or Security Advisory interface
for this repository. Include:

- affected repository, branch, commit and artifact;
- affected stage and invariant identifiers;
- the smallest reproducible counterexample or failure schedule;
- impact and prerequisite trust boundary;
- any known safe containment or corrective change.

Do not attach live secrets or production customer data. Replace them with
synthetic reproductions.
